Privacy Policy for RemiMinder

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Permissions We Use

Camera

We use your camera to scan medication labels and prescriptions to help set up medication reminders.

Microphone / Record Audio

We use your microphone to allow voice input for setting medication reminders and speech-to-text features.

Internet & Network Access

We use internet and network access to sync your reminders, communicate with our servers, and deliver notifications.

Storage (Read & Write)

We use storage access to save and retrieve medication information, audio files, and app data on your device.

Data Collection

We collect only the information necessary to provide medication reminder services. This includes medication names, reminder schedules, and voice recordings used solely for transcription. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data Security

All data is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption. We store your data on secure Google Cloud servers.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: team@remiminderai.com
